Questions

Is the solution safe on suede?

Sharpe Solution is formulated for leather and canvas. For suede and nubuck, we recommend a dry brush pass only — skip the solution and use the bristles alone.

How many cleans per refill?

Each 15mL fill covers approximately 40 spot-cleans, depending on stain size. Refill bottles are sold separately and screw directly into the cap.

Can I wash the wipe panel?

Yes — pop the microfiber panel out from behind the side cover cap and machine wash it on a gentle, cold cycle. Air dry before reinstalling.

What's your return policy?

Return any unused or lightly-used Sharpe Compact within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Reach out to hello@sharpecompact.com to start a return.
